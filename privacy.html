Poochi Analytics - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

Poochi Analytics is a private application designed to help manage and analyze the Poochi & Friends TikTok account. It is not a public analytics service.

2. Information Accessed

With account authorization, the application may access TikTok profile information, public video information, follower counts, following counts, likes, and video statistics.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used for account management, content review, performance analysis, and understanding audience engagement.

4. Data Sharing

Poochi Analytics does not sell TikTok account information or use it for third-party advertising.

5. Data Storage and Security

Access to account information is restricted to authorized users. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect stored information and authentication credentials.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information is retained only as needed for the application's management and analytics purposes. Authorized users may request deletion of stored account information.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses TikTok's developer services. TikTok's own privacy practices are governed by its privacy policy.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or data practices.
